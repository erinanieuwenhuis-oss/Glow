Component 1: Oral Care

Purpose. Catalogue teeth health and progress over time, alongside the products used, with a focus on tooth sensitivity and product ingredients.

Placement. New category in the top panel beside Facial, Body and Hair.

Product catalogue

Each oral care product (toothpaste, mouthwash, floss, whitening, sensitivity treatments and so on) has these fields.

FieldTypeNotes
Product nameTextRequired
BrandTextConsistent with the brand field in other sections
Product typeSelectToothpaste, mouthwash, floss, whitening, other
Full ingredients listLong textRequired; listed as entered
Key activesTagsFor example fluoride, potassium nitrate, hydroxyapatite; searchable and filterable
Ingredients to watchTagsIngredients to avoid or monitor
Important notesTextShown in a different colour in the daily checklist, same behaviour as other sections
Date startedDate
StatusSelectIn use, finished, stopped

Sensitivity tracker

Logged entries that show progress over time.

FieldTypeNotes
DateDate
Sensitivity levelScale 0–100 is none, 10 is severe
TriggersMulti-selectCold, hot, sweet, acidic, brushing, air, other
AreaSelectUpper or lower; left, right or front
NotesText
Products in useLinkedAuto-filled from products marked "In use"

Progress view

  • Line chart of sensitivity level over time
  • Markers on the chart when a product was started or stopped
  • Filter by product, trigger or date range
  • Summary of average sensitivity before and after a product was introduced

Ingredient comparison

  • Select two or more products to compare their ingredients side by side
  • Highlights shared ingredients and ingredients that appear in only one product
  • Flags any product containing an ingredient tagged "Ingredients to watch"

Daily checklist integration

  • Oral care items appear in the daily checklist, morning and evening
  • Product notes display in the highlight colour as a reminder
  • Products can be picked from the oral care stock list when building a day's selection

Component 2: Wash Tools

Purpose. Catalogue the tools used to wash the body (loofah, cloth, sponge, brush and so on), record whether each made a difference, and track when it is time to replace them.

Placement. Sub-section within the Body category.

Fields

FieldTypeNotes
Product nameTextRequired
Tool typeSelectLoofah, washcloth, sponge, brush, mitt, other
Where purchasedTextStore or website; required
Price paidNumber (currency)Required
Date purchasedDateUsed to calculate replacement
Date first usedDate
Replace everyNumber (weeks)Suggested defaults by tool type, editable
Replacement dueAuto dateDate first used plus the replace-every interval
StatusSelectIn use, due for replacement, retired
Made a difference?SelectYes, somewhat, no, too early to tell
ObservationsLong textWhat changed: texture, breakouts, dryness and so on
Important notesTextShown in the highlight colour in the daily checklist

Replacement reminders

  • Status changes to "Due for replacement" automatically when the due date passes
  • The daily checklist shows a highlighted reminder when a tool is due or within 7 days of due
  • Marking a tool as replaced archives it and prompts to add the new tool, pre-filling name, store and price from the previous entry

Effectiveness review

  • Each tool has review entries: date, rating (1–5) and a verdict on whether it made a difference
  • A history view compares tools used over time, including cost per week of use (price divided by weeks used)

Data model additions

OralProduct {
  id, name, brand, type, ingredients, keyActives[], watchIngredients[],
  notes, dateStarted, status
}

SensitivityEntry {
  id, date, level (0-10), triggers[], area, notes, productIds[]
}

WashTool {
  id, name, type, purchasedFrom, price, datePurchased, dateFirstUsed,
  replaceEveryWeeks, replacementDue, status, madeDifference,
  observations, notes
}

WashToolReview {
  id, toolId, date, rating (1-5), verdict, notes
}
