Glow Skin Care – Change Request

Category: Facial  |  Changes 6 & 7  |  Prepared 28 September 2026  |  Purpose: update the current version of the app

Change 6 – Facial Mask Calendar

Location: Facial category, as a new calendar display.

Goal: Track each day a mask is used, which mask it was, and what feature or concern it was used for.

Calendar behaviour

  • Monthly calendar view with previous/next month navigation; today is highlighted.
  • Days with a mask entry show a marker (colour-coded by feature, e.g. hydration, clarifying, brightening, soothing).
  • Tapping a day opens that day's entries and an "Add mask entry" form; entries can be edited or deleted.
  • Multiple masks can be logged on the same day.
  • Filter by mask or by feature to see how often each has been used.

Fields per mask entry

FieldDetails
DateDefaults to selected calendar day
Mask (product name)Selectable from the Facial stock list, or typed in
BrandSame brand field used across all product sections
Feature / purposeWhat it was used for (hydrating, clarifying, brightening, soothing, etc.)
Duration left onOptional, in minutes
Important notesShown in a different colour in the daily checklist as a reminder, same as all other products
ObservationSkin reaction or result after use

Linked behaviour

  • Masks come from the Facial stock component, so owned products can be selected rather than retyped.
  • A mask logged for today appears in that day's checklist, with its important note in the reminder colour.

Change 7 – Gua Sha & Roller Component

Location: Facial category, as its own component.

Goal: Display and update the gua sha and roller routines to follow, usually supplied as a photo, then track completion and visual progress.

Routine library

  • Add a routine by uploading a photo (or several), with a routine name and a tool type: Gua sha Roller Both.
  • Optional fields: description or steps, target area (jawline, cheeks, forehead, neck), duration, and how many times per week.
  • Routines can be updated later: replace the photo, edit details, or archive an old routine.

Selected routine

  • One routine is set as the active one; its photo and details display prominently at the top of the component.
  • Switching the active routine keeps the history of the previous one.

Completion tracking

  • "Mark as done" button on the active routine, with a date-stamped log of each completion.
  • Completion also appears in the daily checklist for the day it was done.
  • Streak and weekly count against the routine's target frequency.

Photo progress tracker

  • Add progress photos over time (date, optional note), stored per routine.
  • Timeline or before/after slider comparing the first photo with the latest.
  • Progress summary: start date, number of photos, number of completed sessions, and days elapsed.
  • Optional observation entry per photo (e.g. puffiness, definition, glow).

Acceptance Checklist

  1. Mask calendar appears under Facial and shows entries by day.
  2. A mask entry stores mask, brand, feature, notes and observation.
  3. Important notes display in the reminder colour in the daily checklist.
  4. Gua sha / roller component appears under Facial with an active routine photo.
  5. Routines can be added, updated and switched.
  6. Completion is logged and visible in the checklist.
  7. Progress photos are stored with dates and can be compared over time.
